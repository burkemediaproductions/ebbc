# Elevated Beauty By Crystal — Homepage Graphic Direction

## Highest-priority original photography

1. **Hero nail-art portrait** — a vertical close-up of Crystal working on a client's nails, photographed against a dark or neutral background. Leave negative space around the hands so it can fit inside the arched hero crop.
2. **Crystal at work** — a polished portrait of Crystal looking toward camera or working at her station. Use this in the “personal beauty experience” section instead of the temporary storefront photo.
3. **Signature nail set** — 3–5 coordinated close-ups showing variety: refined neutral, dramatic dark, gold detail, dimensional art, and a soft romantic look.
4. **Skin-care detail** — hands applying a mask, warm towels, glass bowls, or a close-up treatment moment. Avoid generic stock portraits.
5. **Studio atmosphere** — wide and detail photographs of the Afterglow space, including Crystal's station, products, textures, and signage.

## Custom graphics that match the new page

- **EB circular monogram:** thin antique-gold “EB” with “by Crystal” in script beneath it. Supply as transparent SVG and PNG.
- **Botanical line-art set:** 3–4 delicate gold botanical illustrations that echo the existing logo without copying the full lace border everywhere.
- **Arched portrait masks:** reusable tall arch and oval frames for service photography.
- **Fine gold divider:** a thin asymmetrical flourish for section transitions and social graphics.
- **Nail silhouette illustrations:** elegant hand and nail line drawings for service categories, used very sparingly.
- **Subtle texture:** black paper, warm ivory paper, and champagne metallic grain at low opacity.

## Social and website crossover assets

Create a coordinated set so the site and social media feel like one brand:

- “Nails are my love language” feature graphic
- New-client appointment announcement
- Custom press-on nail feature
- Monthly availability card
- Service spotlight templates for nails, skin care, waxing, and bodywork
- Before-and-after frame with minimal gold labeling
- Instagram story cover icons using the EB monogram and line-art symbols

## Photography treatment

Use warm highlights, rich blacks, softened skin tones, and restrained champagne-gold accents. Avoid strong pink filters, overly bright salon stock photography, and placing long text directly over busy images.

## Temporary images currently used in the build

The homepage currently uses Crystal's nail-tool photograph in the hero and the Afterglow storefront in the studio section. These keep the page functional, but both should eventually be replaced with the hero nail-art portrait and a strong portrait of Crystal described above.
